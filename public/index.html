Evaluation Details

Metrics & Scoring

Rotation and translation measure pose error; Spatial AbsRel measures numerical estimation error. Mask IoU measures functional-region overlap, and Contact measures whether a predicted point lies in the usable region. Pass@1 is success in one complete episode. Manipulation reports baseline success over 182 binary tasks; the arena’s 183 manipulation cases also include one continuous-reward task. Missing records count as unsuccessful assignments.

Protocol & Budgets

Non-tracing Geometry, Spatial Reasoning, and Affordance use five repetitions per case (three for Fable). Tracing, Task Planning, and Manipulation use one reported episode per assignment. Continuous errors use valid estimates; success rates retain failed and missing predictions. Budgets vary by source: for example, RoboCasa allows 60 minutes and 24 model rounds; BEHAVIOR-1K allows 180 minutes and 24 rounds. These are ceilings, not observed completion times.
