Protocol, Metrics & Model Versions

Metrics & Scoring

Rotation and translation measure pose error; AbsRel measures numerical spatial error. Box–mask IoU and Polygon IoU score region overlap on the same 70 Affordance cases using separate box and polygon outputs. Planning covers 157 cases; Manipulation covers 182 binary assignments, plus one continuous-reward case in the inventory. Failed and missing outcomes count as unsuccessful.

Protocol & Budgets

Ten models share the assigned tasks, native interfaces and success criteria. Manipulation retains each batch’s recorded RGB schedule and execution limits. Compare the recorded settings ↗
