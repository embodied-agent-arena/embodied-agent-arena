Introducing GeoProbe

Separate motion
from viewpoint.
Make scale explicit.

GeoProbe adds 168 cases to examine the metric ambiguities behind geometric estimates. Controlled Blender scenes vary camera motion, object motion, and scale references; real-scene images complement these probes.

Fixed-camera, small-shift, and large-shift conditions distinguish object displacement from apparent motion caused by the camera.

Towards robot generalists

Local progress must survive
the next action.

Estimating a scene, finding a contact, or moving an object can succeed while the complete task still fails. The next challenge is to coordinate these abilities, preserve achieved conditions, and reliably finish robotic goals.
